05A mailbox for your agent

Your agentgets mail.You sign off.

A letter lands at agent@yourdomain. The agent reads it, drafts a reply, and holds it until you say send.

Connect Claude Code in one line

claude mcp add --transport http mailyard https://<your-mailyard>/mcp --header "Authorization: Bearer sk_…"

The key is scoped to that agent's mailbox. It reads, searches, drafts and sends over MCP.

For agents

  • Its own mailbox on your domain and its own scoped key
  • Reads, searches, drafts and sends over MCP
  • Send limits and allow-lists per agent, with sends held for your approval

  • Reads attachments, turned into text by Workers AI
